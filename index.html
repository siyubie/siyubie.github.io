About Me

I am currently a joint Ph.D. candidate in Finance and Business Statistics at the Department of Decision Analytics and Operations, City University of Hong Kong, and in Statistics at the School of Statistics, East China Normal University. My research focuses on FinTech, empirical asset pricing, and financial econometrics. During my Ph.D. studies, I am also affiliated with the Laboratory for AI-Powered Financial Technologies (AIFT), where I translate academic insights into FinTech applications, powered by large language models for financial analytics.

Email: siyubie2-c@my.cityu.edu.hk | Google Scholar | SSRN
